# OSRSBossSimulator

### Mission Statement

- Curious players may want to know highly specific boss-related statistics. For instance:
    - How long (assuming 80% of EHB rate) does it take to greenlog a given boss, ignoring cosmetic drops?
    - What is the expected KC at which you obtain at least one ENH and six Armor Seeds from the Corrupted Gauntlet?
    - How likely are you to finish Ballista before receiving four zenytes?
    - What is the likelihood that you obtain Ahrim's top/bottom while farming one complete Barrow's set for the Morytania elite diary? (TENTATIVE)


#### Features
- Simulates your boss farming outcome and renders a timeline to visualise your results.
- Generate average results
